EliteView Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026

EliteView (Package: com.rishi.eliteview) values your privacy. We strictly adopt an on-device architecture:

1. Storage Permissions

The app requests media read and write permissions (READ_MEDIA_VIDEO, READ_MEDIA_AUDIO, READ_MEDIA_IMAGES) strictly to discover, index, and play audio and video stored on your device, and to write converted PDF documents into your local storage.

2. Zero Cloud Transmissions

None of your personal media, thumbnails, or generated PDFs are transmitted to any cloud servers, databases, or analytics engines.

3. Background & Floating Playback

The FOREGROUND_SERVICE and SYSTEM_ALERT_WINDOW permissions are utilized exclusively to facilitate uninterrupted background audio and picture-in-picture pop-up windows when multitasking.
